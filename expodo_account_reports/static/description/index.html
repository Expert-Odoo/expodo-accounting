Accounting Reports for Community

Balance sheet, profit and loss, cash flow, ledgers, aged balances and your country's tax return. Interactive, exportable, with one click from any figure to the entries behind it.

How it works

Open a statement, set the period, read the figures. Unfold any line to see the accounts it is made of, then click an amount to reach the journal entries behind it. Add the previous period or the previous year as an extra column. Export to PDF or Excel, where the amounts arrive as numbers ready to be recomputed.

The reports read your existing chart of accounts and the report definitions your localisation already provides, so they work as installed, in any country. Your tax return is your localisation's own, in its official layout. The balance sheet and profit and loss follow a statutory presentation where one is available: a French company reads its plan comptable général layout, a West African one its SYSCOHADA format. Everywhere else a universal presentation applies, built on the account types Odoo already uses, so the statements are readable from the first day.

Where a statement exists in several presentations, a selector in the header switches between them without leaving the screen: the universal layout, your country's statutory one, and, in France, the intermediate management balances.

Installation

The statements appear under Invoicing › Expodo Reports. Every accounting role reaches them, read-only auditors included. Available in English, French, Spanish, German, Dutch, Italian and Portuguese.

Looking for year-end closing, fixed assets, reconciliation, budgets or customer follow-up as well? Accounting for Community installs this module together with the rest of the suite.
