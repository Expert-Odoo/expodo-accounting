FEC Export

The French accounting entries file, in the format the tax authority expects.

How it works

Choose a period and the file is written: eighteen fields in the prescribed order, tab-separated, dates as YYYYMMDD, decimal commas, and the file named after your company registration number.

Opening balances firstPlaced at the head of the file, as required
Sub-ledger accountsFilled for partner accounts, left empty elsewhere
Foreign currencyReported where it genuinely applies
Checks before writingDraft entries, missing registration number, period gaps
